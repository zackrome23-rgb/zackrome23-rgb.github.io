Privacy Policy

All Roads Lead to Rome LLC, a Florida limited liability company
Effective date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how All Roads Lead to Rome LLC ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit allroadsleadtoromellc.com, email us, or exchange text messages with us.

Information We Collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, mobile phone number, and the content of messages you send us, including scheduling details.
  • SMS consent records: the date, time and method by which you agreed to receive text messages from us, and any opt-out (STOP) or help (HELP) requests.
  • Basic technical information: when you visit our website, our hosting provider may automatically log standard information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view, for security and to keep the site working.

How We Use Information

We use your information to respond to you, schedule appointments, send reminders and replies you asked for, provide customer support, keep records of your consent and opt-out choices, keep our website secure, and meet legal obligations.

SMS and Mobile Information

We do not share, sell, rent or trade mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data and consent with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from every category of sharing described in this Privacy Policy and will not be shared with any third parties.

We share mobile information only with service providers that deliver text messages on our behalf (such as our messaging platform and wireless carriers), solely to send the messages you asked to receive, and when the law requires it.

When We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • with service providers that help us run our business (such as email, messaging and website hosting), who may use it only to provide services to us;
  • when required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect our rights, property or safety or that of others; and
  • with a successor if our business is merged or sold, subject to this Privacy Policy.

Your Choices

  • Text messages: reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.
  • Access, correction or deletion: email us to ask for a copy of your information or to correct or delete it. We may keep records we're required to keep by law, including records of your SMS consent and opt-out.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Children

Our website and messaging program are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
